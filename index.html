I am currently a Ph.D student majoring in Computer Science at Tsinghua University (THU) under the supervision of Prof. Baining Guo. I am also a Research Scientist Intern at Meshy.ai. Besides, I have been a research intern at Microsoft Research Asia (MSRA) since Jul 2021, working closely with Sicheng Xu, Jiaolong Yang and Xin Tong.

My research lies at the intersection of computer vision, graphics and generative AI. I aim to build spatially intelligent AI that can perceive, generate and interact with the 3D world, spanning scalable 3D generative models, geometry perception and world generation.

Beyond research, I am a tinkerer at heart: I write shaders for Minecraft and do electronic designs occasionally. To me, creating is fun in itself, and what we create should bring fun too, whether it lives in a virtual world or comes off a 3D printer. I hope AI can help everyone create for fun, and create fun.
